The eyebrow holes

Why they can't be patched, and why solidifying sidesteps them

Hole filling only works on a rim that is a simple closed loop. We profiled the rims in each export: in B almost none are. The eyebrow holes are bounded by junctions where hair sheets cross each other, which is why the library's own fill, a CPU fill inside the exporter, and a fill on the finished file all leave them open; the first two also drove the exporter's next stage past the box's 91 GB memory cap and got killed. Solidifying does not patch the surface at all. It closes the model's occupancy grid (every strand within two voxels of another joins it, every gap narrower than about four voxels fills), then builds one new surface from that. The torn sheets never exist, so there is nothing to fill: 332 pieces instead of 5,808, and the remesh step is skipped, which is where the other half of the export time went.

A junction vertex is a rim vertex with more than two boundary edges: two sheets meeting at a torn edge. A fill can cap a loop; it cannot decide which of three sheets a junction belongs to. D closes rims by growing surface between hairs, and its texture bake then fails on that new surface: 57% of the texels inside D's UV islands came back with alpha under 50%, versus 0% for B, and the app draws those as grey. That is the haze; it is in the file, not the viewer. pymeshfix was tried on B and did not finish in five minutes; it is built for closed solids and would cap a mustache's outline the same as a hole.

What "closer to the image" means here

Two dials, one of them free

The triangle budget is the holes

Going from 40k to 120k triangles recovers most of the missing hair (84% → 93% kept) for one environment change and about 30 extra seconds per facet. The download grows from 2.6 MB to 6.6 MB; facets download once and are cached on the device.

The 1536 cascade is the fidelity

TRELLIS's 1536 path generates two and a half times the raw geometry, and 96% of it survives at 120k. It costs 77 extra seconds of generation and about 1 GB more VRAM, both well inside the box's limits, but it halves the forge's throughput, and two queued jobs would push the second past the 380-second edge budget. Fine as the default while volume is low; worth a per-request flag later.

Steps: 18 is the knee

Twelve to eighteen steps recovers hair and halves the floating chips for 19 extra seconds; eighteen to twenty-five adds little and doubles the time. A sharper texture and a more aggressive hole fill both crash the current mesh library. The remesh resolution is already at the model's own 1024.

Solidify: the third dial

An environment value on the box sets the closing radius in voxels: 2 today, 0 turns it off and the forge exports through the remesh path as before. If the solidify step fails for any reason the job also falls back to that path, so nothing can get stuck on it. Nothing changes in the app; new facets simply arrive solid. A radius of 3 fills a little more but starts to blunt fine detail, which is why S2 is the default.
